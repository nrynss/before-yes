# P3: Pipeline (Track B)

```yaml
id:       P3
size:     L
requires: [P1, T0.4]
blocks:   P4 jobs, P6
parallel: medium
runs-parallel-with: P2, P4, P5
```

**Goal:** Turn a title and an age into a card that holds up: gather the evidence, read every source into quoted findings with Nano, judge the checklist with Super, find safer alternatives, and prove the result on the three demo titles.

**Where the product lives:** Reading everything and linking every claim to its source is what separates Before Yes from a chatbot. This phase is that difference. Prompt and judgement tasks are frontier work, verified by measuring against the fixtures, not by reading the output and finding it plausible.

Every model and Tavily call goes through `pipeline.Meter`. Tests use a fake meter that records spend per pool and kind.

### T3.1: Gather ★
```yaml
requires:   T1.3, T2.2, T2.3, T0.4a
fixture-ok: yes
size:       M · frontier
owns:       internal/pipeline/gather.go, internal/pipeline/gather_test.go, tools/capture/
status:     done
```
A fixed source plan per title, about eight Tavily credits:
1. App Store search. If a hit's name starts with the title, read its reviews and description, and take its seller URL as the official host. A near miss is worse than no reviews, so a non-matching hit is ignored. **Apple's review feed has been returning no entries for every app** (see the handoff log). When it is empty, fall back to the reviews on the title's App Store web page (T3.8). Try the feed first, in case it returns.
2. Privacy policy and terms searches, narrowed to the official host when one is known. Extract the top hit of each.
3. **Reddit, wider.** Two searches on `reddit.com`: one for parents' experiences and one for the title's own community (chat, scams, moderation). Tavily returns no thread text (`raw_content` is null on every hit), and Reddit refuses Tavily's extract. Each hit's `content` snippet is therefore its own document, with kind `reddit` and the thread URL. A snippet is verbatim text, so its quotes pass the check. Strip the page chrome ("Skip to main content", "Open menu", "Log In") before reading.
4. Forum search (Common Sense Media, Mumsnet, Netmums), news search (fines, lawsuits, regulators, breaches), and rating search (ESRB, PEGI, parental controls), with raw content capped per document.

Common Sense's parent and kid reviews are not a source. Tavily cannot read them, even with the advanced extract, and Common Sense offers no public review API (T0.4a and T0.4b). Its pages may still turn up in the forum search as ordinary documents. Its "Parents say" summary is generated by Common Sense, not written by a parent, and must never be quoted as a parent's report. Google Play stays out: it has no official review API, and the brief rules out scraping.

Record the source kinds that came back empty, for the card's small print.

**Known problems from the T0.4 fixtures** ([review](adversarial-review/t0.4-round1.md)). Solve these here:
- **The official host is missing for half the titles.** Minecraft's App Store hit has no seller URL. Toca's store name ("Toca Boca World: Game & Play") fails the prefix rule. Find the developer's host another way. One option: take the domain of the policy search's top hit when it matches the App Store developer name. Show that it does not admit a look-alike domain.
- **Policy text can be missing.** Khan Academy Kids' policy and terms searches both return the homepage, and Tavily's extract fails on it. When the top hit is a homepage or fails to extract, try the next hit, or a link named privacy or terms. The card must say so when no policy text could be read; it must not grade the data area as if the policy said nothing.
- **Credits:** trust Tavily's `usage.credits`, not the price list.
- **A missing page can look like a success.** Tavily returned Common Sense 404 pages ("Oops! Looks like that page doesn't exist.") as successful results, with nothing in `failed_results` (T0.4b). Detect error and not-found pages by their content before reading them, and count them as missing sources.
- `tools/capture` copies `plan()`. Once `internal/pipeline` exists, make the capture tool use it.

**Done when:** Against the T0.4 and T0.4a fixtures, each demo title gathers the expected documents, Reddit snippets included. The policy and terms searches are narrowed to the official host. Credits match the plan.

**Implementation notes (T3.1).**
- `Gatherer.Gather(ctx, title, ref)` runs `Plan(title, host)`: seven basic searches (policy, terms, two Reddit, forum, news, rating) and at most one extract call, plus one more only when a homepage's privacy or terms links are followed. That is at most eight credits at list price for the usual plan. The extract is conditional: with no policy or terms candidate at all, it is skipped, which is why Minecraft and Khan Academy Kids run at seven credits on the fixtures. Every call goes through `Meter.Spend` on `spend.PoolTavily`, booked under the check ref, and Tavily's reported `usage.credits` settles the charge.
- Official host: the seller URL's registrable domain. When there is none, a policy hit's registrable domain is accepted only when an App Store listing names exactly that domain as its seller URL, and that listing is tied to the title. With an app matched by name, the listing must have the same developer: Minecraft Education by Mojang AB names education.minecraft.net, so minecraft.net passes. With no app matched, the listing's developer name must be the domain's name and its store name must start with the title's first word (Toca Boca AB, tocaboca.com, "Toca Boca World"); that listing becomes the app. Refused in tests: `minecraft.wiki`, `minecraft.co.uk`, `mojang.xyz`, `roblox.app`, `tocaboca.co.uk`, `tocabocashop.com`, `tocaboca.helpshift.com`, `tocaboca.com.evil.io`, `toca-boca.com`, and another developer's own `pazugames.com`.
- Policy and terms: the first two hits on the host that are not homepages go into one extract call, kind-named hits first (four URLs still fit one credit). The first page read that is not an error page and names its kind in its URL, title, or first 300 characters is kept. Otherwise the kind is missing, with a note.
- Reddit snippets are documents of kind `reddit`, with the chrome stripped. Common Sense "Parents say" and "Kids say" lines are dropped. Error and not-found pages are counted as missing. Raw content is capped at 60000 characters.
- Fixture results: Roblox has no policy (its policy hits are the download page and homepages). Minecraft has no policy or terms (the only policy hit on its host is the usage guidelines, and the EULA was never captured). Khan Academy Kids has no policy or terms (homepage only). Toca has both.
- `Doc` lives in gather.go, not doc.go, because T3.1 produces it first.
- **Owns widened, approved by the user:** T3.1 now owns `tools/capture/` as well. `tools/capture` builds its searches from `pipeline.Plan` (main capture and the `-only reddit` run), so its own copy of the plan is gone. Its request-body test now expects the seven planned searches and eight credits.

### T3.2: Reader (Nano) ★
```yaml
requires:   T1.2, T3.1
fixture-ok: yes
size:       L · frontier
owns:       internal/pipeline/doc.go, internal/pipeline/read.go, internal/pipeline/read_test.go
status:     done
```
**Chunking.** Token Factory serves Nano with a 32k context and 4k output tokens. Reviews share a chunk. A long document is split on paragraph or sentence boundaries, with a small overlap and rune-safe cuts, and every piece keeps its document's ref. Each segment is labelled `[R312]` for a review or `[D3]` for a document.

**Prompt.** One finding per claim, each with a ref, area, claim, stance, and a 10 to 40 word verbatim quote. No outside knowledge. Skip bug reports and gameplay talk. Mark first-hand reports as such in the claim. Thinking off: it is billed as output.

**Verification.** Every finding is checked against the whole text of the document its ref names (T1.2). A ref outside the chunk counts as unquoted. Calls run with bounded concurrency. A chunk whose call fails is counted and skipped. A check fails only if every chunk fails.

**Done when:** With recorded Nano replies for the fixtures, the review 312 equivalent in the "no" title survives as a risk finding, every invented quote is dropped, and drop counts are reported by reason.

**Implementation notes (T3.2).**
- `Reader.Read(ctx, title, ref, docs)` returns `Read{Findings, Drops, Chunks, Failed, Cost}`. Every call goes through `chat()`, which uses `Meter.Spend` on `spend.PoolModel` with kind `<provider>:read`. The card cost is what the meter settled at.
- Each chunk's reply is parsed, and any ref outside that chunk counts as unquoted. Then one `finding.Verify` runs over all chunks against every document's whole text, so duplicates collapse across chunks.
- Concurrency is bounded (default 4). A failed call, or a reply that will not parse, is counted in `Failed` and skipped. Read errors only when every chunk fails.
- `split` cuts always land on rune boundaries, including the overlap start; a test exercises this with multibyte text that has no spaces.
- The "recorded Nano replies" here are hand-written replies inside read_test.go, served by a fake model keyed by segment ref. Real recordings belong to T3.6b (testdata/eval/replies/), which needs keys. Against the Roblox fixtures, review 355 (the fixtures' review-312 equivalent) survives as a contact risk. Invented quotes, a ref from another chunk and a ref no document has are all dropped as unquoted; bad area, bad stance, short quote and duplicate drops are counted.
- `Chatter` (the model surface) and `chat()` live in read.go and are reused by the judge.
- **Stand-in replies, accepted by the user:** the recorded replies in read_test.go are hand-written stand-ins, not real model output. T3.6b must replace them with real recordings from `testdata/eval/replies/`.

**Round 1 remediation (T3.2).**

| ID | Fix | Test |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | `assignRefs` labels every doc uniquely. A review is `R<index>` the first time its index appears; a later review with the same index takes `D<i+1>`. Chunking and verification use the same labels, so T3.8's store-page reviews cannot collide with the feed's. | `TestReviewRefsUnique` |
| L2 | Not enforced, by design. The spec puts the 10 to 40 word range in the prompt; verification (T1.2) only requires `finding.MinQuote` normalized runes. ### T3.3: Judge (Super) and the conflict pass ★
```yaml
requires:   T1.1, T3.2
fixture-ok: yes
size:       L · frontier
owns:       internal/pipeline/judge.go, internal/pipeline/judge_test.go
status:     done
```
Super receives the child's age, the six area questions, and every verified finding: id, area, stance, source kind or review number, claim, and quote. It returns one grade per area, citing finding ids. A grade cannot cite anything else.

The prompt states the weighting rule from the brief: a first-hand report of what happened outweighs what a policy or store listing claims, and one credible report that a filter or age gate failed for a young child can decide an area.

**Conflict pass.** An area with both a risk and a mitigation finding is re-judged alone, with thinking on. The second grade replaces the first only for the areas it answers. If the second pass fails, the first grades stand.

Every grade then goes through `checklist.Enforce`, and the verdict comes from `checklist.Decide`.

**Done when:** With recorded Super replies, the "no" title's contact area is flagged on the review 312 finding, the conflict pass runs only on conflicted areas, and a grade citing a made-up id is demoted.

**Implementation notes (T3.3).**
- `Judge.Judge(ctx, title, age, ref, findings, missing)` returns `Judgement{Grades, Demotions, Verdict, Conflicted, ConflictErr, Cost}`.
- The first pass uses JSON mode with `Fast` merged in. Areas that have both a risk and a mitigation finding are re-judged alone (prompt line "Grade only these areas") with `Think` merged in and a 16000-token cap. Its grades replace the first-pass grades only for those areas. When it fails (a call error or no grades in the reply), the first-pass grades stand and the reason is kept in `ConflictErr`.
- After that, `checklist.Enforce` runs with `finding.Cited`, then `checklist.Decide`. Both calls are metered (`<provider>:judge` and `<provider>:judge_think`).
- The prompt lists unread source kinds and says their silence is not evidence, so the data area is not graded `ok` on a policy that was never read.
- `parseGrades` tries every opening brace, so it copes with reasoning text and code fences in front of the answer.
- The recorded Super replies are hand-written in judge_test.go, against real verified Roblox finding ids. The contact flag rests on review 355. The conflict pass runs only for contact. A money flag citing a made-up id is demoted to unknown.
- **Stand-in replies, accepted by the user:** the recorded replies in judge_test.go are hand-written stand-ins, not real model output. T3.6b must replace them with real recordings from `testdata/eval/replies/`.

| L2 | Accepted by the user: the judge is tested on hand-written stand-in replies until T3.6b records real ones. | stand-in note above |

### T3.4: Collect, decide, and the evidence cache ★
```yaml
requires:   T3.2, T3.3
fixture-ok: yes
size:       M · mid
owns:       internal/pipeline/pipeline.go, internal/pipeline/pipeline_test.go
status:     done
```
Split the run into `Collect(title) → Evidence` and `Decide(evidence, age) → Card`. Collect is where the Tavily credits and most model tokens go. It is cached by title, plan version, storefront and reader model, so asking about the same title for another age costs one judge call.

The card cites the findings its grades rest on, plus every risk finding, so a parent can see everything the judge weighed. Stats record read and judge cost separately.

**Done when:** An end-to-end test with fakes shows review 312 deciding the card. A second age on the same evidence makes no reader or Tavily call. Cost on the card equals what the meter recorded.

**Implementation notes (T3.4).**
- `Pipeline{Gatherer, Reader, Judge, Cache, Storefront}`.
  - `Collect(ctx, title, ref) → (Evidence, hit, error)`.
  - `Decide(ctx, ev, age, ref) → Card`.
  - `Run(ctx, Request, ref)`, with `Request.Normalize` checking ages 3 to 17 and titles up to 120 characters.
- `EvidenceKey(title, storefront, readerModel)` contains the folded title, `PlanVersion`, the storefront and the reader model. `EvidenceCache` is an interface with an in-memory `MemCache`; the durable cache with single flight is T4.2. A failed collect is never cached.
- The card shows the findings its grades cite plus every risk finding. `Stats` keeps read and judge cost separate.
  - On a cache hit, the card carries the evidence's read cost as it was collected, and only this decision's judge cost is booked under the new ref.
  - `TavilyCredits` is an int in types.go, so it is rounded.
- Card ids are `c_` followed by 22 base-62 characters from crypto/rand.
- `Kind` is `ios` when an App Store hit matched, and empty otherwise; console and YouTube are later tasks.
- An end-to-end test runs the Roblox fixtures with recorded reader replies and a rule-based judge stand-in. Review 355 (the captures' review-312 equivalent) decides "no"; without it the card is "unclear". A second age makes no Tavily or reader call and one judge call. The card's model cost equals `Spent(PoolModel)`, and its credits equal `Spent(PoolTavily)`.
- **Stand-in replies, accepted by the user:** the recorded replies in pipeline_test.go are hand-written stand-ins, not real model output. T3.6b must replace them with real recordings from `testdata/eval/replies/`.

### T3.5: Safer alternatives ★
```yaml
requires:   T3.4
fixture-ok: yes
size:       L · frontier
owns:       internal/pipeline/alternatives.go, internal/pipeline/alternatives_test.go, plus its hook in internal/pipeline/pipeline.go and an additive card field in internal/pipeline/types.go
status:     done
```
The brief: safer alternatives, found with Tavily and checked the same way. Tavily is load-bearing twice, gathering evidence and finding alternatives, which is the case for the Best Use of Tavily award.

When a card is **no** or **yes with settings**, search for two or three alternatives in the same genre for the same age. Run each candidate through `Collect` and `Decide`, cached like any title. Keep only candidates whose verdict beats the original's. Show each with its verdict and a link to its own card.

Alternatives multiply cost. They are bounded by count, run after the main card is shown, and refused by the meter like anything else. A card is complete without them. It carries only checked alternatives, plus a one-line note when at least one was refused. Pending and refused entries stay internal.

**Done when:** Against fixtures, the "no" title yields at least one alternative with a better verdict and its own card id. A budget refusal leaves the main card intact.

**Implementation notes (T3.5).**
- `alternatives.go` covers two steps.
  - **Finding:** `FindAlternatives(ctx, card, ref)` runs only for a `no` or `yes_with_settings` card. It makes one Tavily search ("safer <genre> like <title> for a <age> year old"; the genre is the App Store genre, or "video game" or "YouTube channel" from the kind), metered under the check's ref. It returns up to `MaxAlternatives` (3) candidates with `status: pending`.
  - **Candidate names** come from hit titles: cut at " | ", " - " or ": ", and drop a trailing "App/Game Review". A list ("10 Best Games Like Roblox", "Top…", "…alternatives…", "… vs …") and the title itself are skipped.
  - **Checking:** `CheckAlternatives` checks each candidate for the card's age through `Collect` (cached like any title) and `Decide`, under the ref `<ref>/alt<n>`. It keeps only a candidate whose verdict beats the original's (yes > yes_with_settings > no; `unclear` never beats). A kept candidate has `status: checked`, its verdict and its own `card_id`, and its card is returned to be stored.
  - **Refusals:** a `cost.ErrOverBudget` from any call stops the run, and the candidates not yet checked are marked `refused`. The daily cap refuses only fresh checks (user decision below). A candidate that fails another way is dropped.
  - `WithAlternatives` runs both steps after the main card is made and returns a copy of the card with the alternatives. On any error, a refused search included, the card comes back unchanged.
- **The P4 hook** is `Pipeline.WithAlternatives`, or `FindAlternatives`, `CheckAlternatives` and `AltResult.Apply` as two steps. The pending list is never shown: a pending name may then be refused by the cap, so a card shown before the checks finish carries no alternatives (round-6 remediation below). pipeline.go is unchanged: `Run` still returns the main card alone.
- **Contract:** `Alternative.Status` (`status`) is new and additive. Internally it is pending, checked or refused. On a card, and so in every API response, it is always `checked`. `card-no.json` and `api/get-card.json` show `"status": "checked"`; the `types_test.go` generator line was updated to match.
- **Fixtures:** the main card is the captured Roblox fixture (review 355 gives "no"). The alternatives search and the candidates' sources are fakes in `alternatives_test.go`: Calm Builder, which answers every area with a setting; Chatty World, which has a contact risk; and Blank Slate, which says nothing. No live call was made.
- **Tests:** `TestNoTitleGetsABetterAlternative` (the Done-when: Calm Builder, yes_with_settings, its own card id; the list and self skipped; the count bounded; each candidate under its own ref; a second run reads nothing again, so the evidence is cached); `TestBudgetRefusalLeavesMainCard` (the Done-when: refused candidate checks are marked refused and the card is otherwise byte-identical; a refused search returns the card unchanged); `TestAlternativesOnlyForNoAndSettings`; `TestCandidateName`.
