## Findings

### H1. The overlap test does not show a reason rests on its quote; invented reasons still stand
`ground` accepts a grade when the reason and a cited verified quote share 2 five-letter stems. Probe (scratch test, not committed):
- Quote: "Great game, my daughter loves building worlds with her friends every single afternoon." (stance risk)
- Reason: "The company sells her data to advertisers and refuses deletion; friends' worlds are exposed."
- Result: `flag` stands, citing that quote. It shares "frien" and "world".

This is the T3.6b failure mode, an invented reason, passing through the guard that was built to stop it. Two shared generic words are not a principled measure of naming a finding. The PR's own open item shows the same thing on the recorded replies: Roblox `developer_record` stands on a forum quote about safety protections, not on a fine or lawsuit. The brief requires zero residue, so this is a real finding, not accepted residue. The rule needs a check a model cannot satisfy by accident. One option: the reason must contain a verbatim span of the cited verified quote (a run of N words, or the whole quote). The prompt and request can't change, so the replay will show which grades that demotes. Report those to the user.

### H2. An ok_with_setting's setting is never checked against a quote
`said` is the reason and the setting merged into one bag of words, so overlap from the reason alone carries the grade. Probe: the quote "The app collects personal data about every account." backs the setting "Turn on Delete My Account in settings" with the reason "It collects personal data; deletion is available." The grade stands. Roblox `data` on the recorded replies is the same case. The PR notes no quote backs its deletion setting. The parent is told to use a control no verified quote names. The setting must be backed by a cited quote on its own, a mitigation quote, or the grade falls to unknown.
