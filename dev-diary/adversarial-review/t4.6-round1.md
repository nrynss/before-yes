## Probes that held

- **The order is right.** The join comes after validation, visitor-header checks and the card cache, and before `admit` (`budget_exhausted`), `StartKind` (`busy`) and, at the gate, `live_checks_off`. A joiner reserves nothing (`TestDoubleClickJoinsRunningCheck`: one reservation held while running, 2 booked in total).
- **Title normalization is neither too loose nor too strict.** The key is `cards.TitleKey` (fold whitespace, lower-case), the same key the card cache uses, plus the age. A different age is a different check. Punctuation and Unicode variants are not merged, which matches the card cache.
- **A failed check leaves no joiner stuck.** `t.done` is deferred around the whole wrapped job, so it runs on error and on panic. In the pin above, the entry was gone once the check failed. Joiners hold the failed job's id and read its `error` terminal.
- **The insert cannot race the job's end.** `done` takes the same lock `startCheck` holds through `StartKind` and the insert, so a job that ends at once removes its entry only after the insert. `done` matches by `ref`, so a stale removal cannot delete a newer entry.
- **Visitors stay separate.** Visitor-key requests (`sent`, even half a pair) never look up, never insert and never pass the gate as joiners. `TestVisitorRequestNeverJoinsOurCheck` and `TestVisitorCheckIsNeverJoined` pin this.
- **The `gate.go` change is minimal.** It adds one predicate and one `||`. It uses the same decode and `Normalize` as `cachedOrInvalid`. Whether it lets non-joiners through is M1.
