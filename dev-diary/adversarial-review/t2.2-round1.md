Pacing judgement. Tavily's development key is documented at 100 requests a minute (`help.tavily.com`, "Rate Limits"). `FreePerMinute` 100 and the paced `PerMinute` 90 are correct for this project's free key: the pacer spaces starts at 60/90 plus a 5% margin, measured at 700ms on the wire, and Keel shares one process-wide pacer per `(name, rate)`, so more than one client in the process still draws on one window. What the pace does not cover is retries: Keel hands a rate slot to the first attempt only, so a 429 or 5xx storm can put more than 90 requests a minute on the wire. The pipeline's plan is five searches and one extract per title, run in sequence, so this is a storm-time exposure, not a routine one. T2.2b can close the question: its live search and extract already record whether 429s appear and whether `usage.credits` comes back. No timed test asserts the spacing in the package; the config value is asserted, and this review measured the spacing outside the package.
