## What was checked
- **Gate order.** `POST /api/checks` = Keel `gate.Protect` (passcode, then the per-client + global buckets under one lock) → live switch → `startCheck`. The live switch passes invalid bodies (400) and fresh cached cards (200) through and refuses only a would-be fresh check. This matches docs/api.md steps 1, 2, 3 and 5. Step 4 (join a running check) is not implemented on `main` either, so it is outside this task.
- **Passcode.** Keel `matches`: a length check, then `subtle.ConstantTimeCompare`. Only the length can leak, and Keel documents that. The header and the cookie are both checked; with no passcode configured, every request passes (T4.4 must resolve `[gate.passcode]`). No bypass found: every other route is ungated, and only `POST /api/checks` is routed through `checks`. The ServeMux pattern also binds the method, so `GET /api/checks/{id}` stays ungated, as intended.
- **Client IP.** Forwarding headers are read only when the peer is in 127/8, ::1 or 172.16/12. XFF takes the rightmost entry, so a forged leftmost entry does not change the key. IPv6 clients are keyed by /64.
- **Cached card while off.** The live switch peeks at the same 4 KB body and runs the same `Normalize` and `CardCache.Fresh` as the handler, so the two cannot disagree on the body. A cache error counts as "not cached", which fails closed.
- **Flag read failure.** It refuses with `live_checks_off` and logs. Fails closed. ✓
- **Rate limits.** Per client: burst 3, then 1 per 2 min. Global: burst 10, then 1 per 30 s. The daily cap of 5 is the real money bound. Sane.

## Findings

### Low (accepted)
- **L1: CF-Connecting-IP is believed from any trusted peer.** Keel prefers `CF-Connecting-IP` over XFF when the peer is trusted. If Traefik passes a client-sent `CF-Connecting-IP` through unchanged (because the origin can be reached without Cloudflare in front), then a client can mint a new per-client bucket on every request. The global bucket (10, then 1 per 30 s) and the daily cap still bound spend, and Keel documents the per-client key as fairness, not security. Accepted. **T6.1** should confirm that Cloudflare fronts the origin, or that Traefik strips `CF-Connecting-IP`, in addition to the 172.16/12 check already noted.
- **L2: two cache reads, so a narrow fail-open window.** `liveSwitch` calls `CardCache.Fresh`, then `startCheck` calls it again. If a card goes stale between the two reads, while the switch is off, one fresh check starts. The window is microseconds, the attacker cannot steer it, and the gate and the daily cap still bound it. Accepted. A later refactor could pass the switch's decision down instead of reading twice.
