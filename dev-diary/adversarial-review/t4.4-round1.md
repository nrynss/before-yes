## Probes that held

- **Routes match the contract.** `GET /api/status`, `GET /api/checklist` and `GET /healthz` are on `App`, and `/api/` goes to the gated API. `TestStatusRoute` and `TestChecklistRoute` compare against the contract examples. `POST /api/status` falls through to the API (404), not the app.
- **Status fails closed.** A throwaway test with a flag store whose `Bool` errors returned `"live":false`.
- **Budget pools are correct.** The model pool limit is `cost.USD(model_usd)`, reported in nanodollars. The Tavily pool is `credits*CreditUnit`, reported divided by `CreditUnit` as whole credits, with its period pinned to MonthlyUTC by `spend`. The checks pool is `Price(n)` with `CheckDenomination` Minor 1 and its period pinned to DailyUTC. Negative remaining is clamped to 0.
- **The SPA fallback does not shadow `/api` or leak files.** A throwaway test put a `secret.txt` beside `web/` and requested it many ways. `/../secret.txt` and `/assets/../../secret.txt` got `307` to the cleaned `/secret.txt`, which serves the shell. `%2e%2e` forms got 400. `/api`, `/api/` and `/api/nope` all reached the API. No response carried the secret. `os.DirFS` also refuses `..`.
- **The serve wiring is complete.** `Judge.Entail` is the reader client in both `serve` and `check`. One `*spend.Meter` is `Server.Checks`, `Pipeline.Checks` and the gatherer, reader and judge meter. The flag store is on the app DB, with `LiveFlag(live)` as the default. The passcode resolves (unset means no passcode). `GatedHandler` is mounted inside `App`. Each is pinned by the main tests.
- **No other component shares a DB with a concurrent writer in-process.** Cards, jobs, the evidence cache and flags all use the one `c.DB` handle. The meter is alone on `spend.db`. The cross-process case is M1.
- **`check` reserves one daily check.** `BeginCheck`, then `Fail` on error and `Done` on a card.
- **The config reads every example key.** `TestConfigCommandAndExample` parses the real example with the strict loader.
- **The additive contract changes are minimal and consistent.** `checklist_version` is in `get-status.json` and `docs/api.md`, and `/healthz` has one doc line. The implementer's own notes flag both as outside owns.

## Residue

M1, L1 and L2 must close in remediation (L2 can go to the orchestrator as a configuration comment). A1 to A4 are recorded as accepted.
